# Guide for the Talent Acquisition team

You sign in at `/wp-admin` on the Voigue website. You can manage hiring and a few content areas. You cannot change the rest of the site, which keeps it safe.

After you sign in you land on **Applications**. Your dashboard shows the number of new applications, open vacancies and messages.

## Applications

Every time someone applies on the Careers page, an entry appears here and an email goes to the careers address.

- **New** applications have a coloured badge and the menu shows how many are new.
- Open an application to see the candidate's details, the role they applied for (or "General CV"), their message, and a **Download CV** button. Set the **status**: New, Reviewing, Shortlisted, Interview, Rejected, Hired.
- To change several at once, tick them, choose **Mark as ...** under Bulk actions, and press Apply.
- Use the status dropdown above the list to filter, and the search box to find a name.
- CVs are private: only signed-in team members can download them. **Do not forward the download link**, and do not save CVs to shared folders.
- **Move to Trash** (and then delete permanently) removes the application **and the CV file**. Delete candidates' data when you no longer need it.
- Applications cannot be added by hand. They only come from the website.

## Vacancies

1. **Vacancies > Add vacancy**.
2. Title: the job title.
3. Big editor: the full description, shown when a visitor presses "Read more".
4. **Vacancy details**: short summary (under 160 characters), work setup (Remote / Hybrid / On-site), location, employment type, key responsibilities and what we're looking for (one per line).
5. **Order** (right-hand side): lowest number shows first.
6. Press **Publish**. It appears on the Careers page straight away.

To close a vacancy, switch it to **Draft** or move it to Trash. Applications already received are kept.

## Messages

**Messages** lists what people sent through the Contact Us form. Open one to read it. Reply from your own email.

## Employee Voices and Life around here

- **Employee Voices**: quotes and photos of team members shown on the Life at Voigue page. Add a voice with the person's name as the title, then their role and testimonial under **Employee details**, and a photo with **Set featured image**.
- **Life around here**: gallery photos. Add a title and photo (use **Set featured image**) and choose a category (Community, Events, Parties). You can choose from the existing categories; only an administrator can add new ones.
- Photos: use JPG/PNG, landscape, 1600 px wide is plenty; keep each under about 500 KB.

## Media

**Media** is the library of photos and videos you have uploaded. You can upload new files; ask an administrator before deleting ones you did not add.

## Good habits

- Sign out when you leave your computer; use a strong, unique password and two-factor authentication if enabled.
- Candidates' details are personal data. Share only with those involved in hiring.
- If something looks wrong or you cannot reach a page you need, contact the website administrator.
