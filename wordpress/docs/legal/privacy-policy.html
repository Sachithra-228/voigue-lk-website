2. What this policy covers

This policy covers personal information collected through this website only. Information about our employees, and the services we provide to our clients, is handled under separate agreements and notices.

3. Information we collect

When you contact us using the Contact Us form: your first and last name, email address, phone number (if you give it) and your message.

When you apply for a role or send us your CV: your name, email address, the role you applied for or your area of interest, your preferred work setup, your message, and your CV file together with the information it contains.

Information collected automatically: like most websites, our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, which can include your IP address, browser type, the pages you request and the time of the request. To protect our forms from spam, we use a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address to limit repeated submissions. It is deleted automatically within an hour and is not linked to your name or application.

We do not ask for sensitive information such as your religion, health, political opinions or national identity number. Please do not include this kind of information in your CV or message unless it is needed for the role.

4. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and keep a record of our conversation.
  • To assess your application, contact you about the role or other suitable roles, and arrange interviews.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent spam and misuse.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Why we are allowed to use it

Under the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022 of Sri Lanka [and, where it applies, the Australian Privacy Act 1988], we use your information because:

  • you have given it to us and asked us to respond to an enquiry or consider your application (consent);
  • it is needed to take steps, at your request, before a possible employment or service contract;
  • it is in our legitimate interests to keep the website secure and to run our recruitment process fairly; and
  • in some cases, we are required to by law.

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time (see section 10).

  • Service providers who help us run the website and our email, such as our website hosting provider [Bluehost] and our email provider [Microsoft 365]. They may only use the information to provide their service to us.
  • [If CVs are shared with client companies: The client company that the role is with, but only after we have discussed the role with you and you have agreed.]

Staff accounts use strong passwords and two-factor authentication. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and review our safeguards regularly.
